Jan 2026 – present

National University of Singapore · Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, Sentaurus TCAD, Verilog-A

What a Hamming-distance search does

A BCAM compares a 32-bit query against every stored vector at once. The stored vector that differs in the fewest bits is the closest match. Click the bits in the top row to change the query.

Projects

PN-junction virtual fabrication and TCAD characterisation

View full project →

Sentaurus Workbench, SProcess, SDevice, SVisual · March 2026

Co-developed a 2D process flow covering STI, well and P+/N+ implants, anneals, contact etch and aluminium metallisation, then handed the structure to SDevice. Sweeping acceptor doping from 1.1e14 to 1.1e16 cm⁻³ shrank the junction depth from 2.526 to 1.816 µm. Higher doping lowered reverse current and raised built-in potential. High-level injection and series resistance limited the high-bias behaviour.

RTL design, verification and synthesis of RISC control and FP8 arithmetic

View full project →

Verilog, Synopsys VCS, Design Compiler, Tcl, Shell · April 2026
