Energy-efficient CMOS, sequential logic and SRAM stability

Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, Monte Carlo

Overview

Sized logic paths using logical effort and energy-delay analysis. Designed a transmission-gate flip-flop, characterised leakage and body-bias effects, and ran 1,000 Monte Carlo simulations. A 45-nm 6T SRAM achieved hold/read/write SNMs of 124.5/102.6/435.0 mV; read stability was limiting.
