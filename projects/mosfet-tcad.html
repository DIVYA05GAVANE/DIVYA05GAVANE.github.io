PN junction, MOS capacitor and MOSFET simulation

Sentaurus SDE, SDevice

Overview

Built device structures and analysed band diagrams, carrier transport and electrostatics. Extracted threshold voltage, transconductance and subthreshold swing from ID-VG curves. Studied doping, oxide thickness, gate work function and temperature, then optimised for low-power gm efficiency.

Tools and methods

Sentaurus SDE, SDevice
