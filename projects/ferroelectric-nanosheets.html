ACADEMIC & ENGINEERING PROJECT

Literature Review: Ferroelectric Nanosheet Devices for Advanced MOS Technologies

Oct 2025 – Nov 2025

Associated with National University of Singapore

Project description

Conducted an in-depth literature review on ferroelectric nanosheet–based MOS devices, focusing on their role in overcoming CMOS scaling limits through gate-all-around (GAA) architectures and ferroelectric gate stacks. Studied ferroelectric nanosheet FETs (Fe-NSFETs), negative capacitance effects, and polarization-controlled operation for low-power and multifunctional electronics.

Analyzed material systems such as HfZrO₂, SrMnO₃, and 2D ferroelectric nanosheets, including growth techniques (MBE, PLD, CVD), strain engineering, and interface control. Evaluated device-level impacts on threshold voltage modulation, subthreshold slope improvement, non-volatile behavior, and signal-processing functionality, connecting material physics to transistor and circuit-level performance.

Identified key challenges and research gaps related to scalability, reliability, parasitics, and CMOS integration, gaining strong exposure to emerging transistor technologies for low-power logic, memory, and neuromorphic computing.
