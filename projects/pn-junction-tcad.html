PN-junction virtual fabrication and TCAD characterisation

Sentaurus Workbench, SProcess, SDevice, SVisual

Overview

Co-developed STI, well formation, P+/N+ implantation, diffusion and annealing, oxide/contact etch, aluminium metallisation and SDevice handoff. Automated TDR checkpoints and junction-depth extraction. An acceptor sweep from 1.1e14 to 1.1e16 cm^-3 reduced Xj from 2.526 to 1.816 micrometres. Analysed band diagrams and I-V behaviour, including high-level injection and series resistance.

Tools and methods
