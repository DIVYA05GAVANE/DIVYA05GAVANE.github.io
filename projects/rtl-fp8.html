RTL design, verification and synthesis of RISC control and FP8 arithmetic

Verilog, Synopsys VCS, Design Compiler, Tcl, Shell

Overview

Implemented and verified a 16-bit RISC program-counter block. Built and regression-tested 3-, 4- and 5-stage FP8 adders with 256 functional vectors. Automated clock sweeps and PPA extraction, reducing the minimum feasible period from 1.0 ns to 0.6 ns; selected the 4-stage design at 1.43x throughput with 45.74% additional area.

Tools and methods

Verilog, Synopsys VCS, Design Compiler, Tcl, Shell
