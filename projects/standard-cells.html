45-nm standard cells and post-layout characterisation

Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, FreePDK45

Overview

Built DRC/LVS-clean INV, NAND2 and NOR2 layouts. Modelled pi-RC interconnects and Elmore delay, characterised FO4 delay and logical effort, and used a ring oscillator plus extraction to quantify parasitic penalties.
