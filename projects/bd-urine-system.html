Electronic Architecture for Becton Dickinson Next-Generation Urine Collection System

Electronic circuit design, Arduino, sensors, Bluetooth

Overview

Designed a compact sensor-driven prototype architecture for a PureWick urine-collection system. Integrated liquid and level sensors, Arduino control, relay pump circuitry, buzzer alerts and Bluetooth communication, with control logic for continuous monitoring and safe actuation.
