ACADEMIC & ENGINEERING PROJECT

Semiconductor Energy Bands & Carrier Statistics Modeling

Oct 2025 – Nov 2025

Associated with National University of Singapore

Project description

Developed physics-based computational models to analyze semiconductor band structure and carrier statistics, combining analytical theory with practical numerical implementation.

Implemented a Kronig–Penney–based model to compute electron, heavy-hole, and light-hole sub-band energy levels in GaAs/Al₀.₃Ga₀.₇As superlattices, studying the effect of quantum well width and barrier thickness on miniband formation and quantum confinement. Generated energy dispersion plots to visualize the transition from isolated quantum wells to coupled superlattice behavior.

Built an interactive Excel-based tool to calculate Fermi level position and carrier concentrations (electrons and holes) in silicon across a wide temperature range (10–800 K). The model dynamically updates carrier densities and Fermi energy with changes in donor and acceptor concentrations, enabling parametric analysis of doping and temperature effects relevant to real semiconductor devices.

This project strengthened my ability to translate semiconductor physics equations into practical computational tools, perform parameter sweeps, visualize physical trends, and connect theoretical models to device-relevant behavior.
