ACADEMIC & ENGINEERING PROJECT

MOS Capacitor Modeling & C–V Characterization

Sep 2025 – Oct 2025

Associated with National University of Singapore

Project description

Modeled a 2D MOS capacitor in Sentaurus TCAD and performed C–V characterization to study accumulation, depletion, and inversion behavior. Analyzed the influence of oxide thickness and substrate doping concentration on flat-band voltage, capacitance variation, and electrostatics.

Compared simulated C–V curves against ideal theoretical behavior to identify non-idealities and device-physics limitations, gaining hands-on experience in parameter sensitivity analysis critical for gate stack and process optimization in CMOS technologies.
