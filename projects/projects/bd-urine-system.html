ACADEMIC & ENGINEERING PROJECT

Designing an electronic architecture for Becton Dickinson (BD’s) Next Generation Urine Collection System

May 2020 – Jul 2020

Associated with BD

Project description

The whole internship project was divided into four phases for better understanding and smooth execution of the Problem Statement.

For the initial stage, I started studying human anatomy, specifically the Human Urinary System and the differences between the male and female urinary system. This helped to better understand the medical condition Urinary Incontinence.

In the second phase, the knowledge about urinary incontinence helped in better understanding BD’s then current product offering i.e. BD PureWick™ Urine Collection System. It has mechanical, electrical and pneumatic architecture, combinedly working as a system. The device consists of the components like Canister (2000 cc): Urine Collection Unit; Female External Catheter (FEC): Catheter for non-invasive urine; Vacuum Station: Diaphragm pump present inside creates a negative pressure/ suction pull in the canister; 16’’ vacuum tubing Creating vacuum in the canister by moving air out from vacuum connector port; 70’’ patient tubing: Carries away urine from the FEC to the canister; Electrical Circuit (PCBA): Provides information on the Battery Life Status using LED light.

In the third phase, I had to design an electronic architecture for a new version of the device, aimed at achieving some improvements: Reducing overall footprint and making the system portable; Designing smart and intuitive system by accurate selection of hardware components and designing of control system circuit to accommodate mentioned features: Switch on Pump only when required, Sensor feedback for continuous tracking of urine level and an alerting system for emptying of urine.

Fourth and final phase involved delivering the project with expected output and presenting the same in front of all board of members involved in designing the Internship. It was a wholesome experience where I developed my interpersonal skills along with the technical learnings.
