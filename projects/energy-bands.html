Semiconductor Energy Bands & Carrier Statistics Modeling

Fermi level calculation, Excel modeling

Overview

Developed physics-based computational models for semiconductor band structure and carrier statistics. Implemented a Kronig-Penney model for GaAs/Al0.3Ga0.7As superlattices and an interactive Excel tool for Fermi level and carrier concentrations in silicon from 10 to 800 K.

Tools and methods

Fermi level calculation, Excel modeling
