ACADEMIC & ENGINEERING PROJECT

BLE controlled obstruction detecting Bot

Oct 2019 – Feb 2020

Associated with MKSSS Cummins College of Engineering for Women

Project description

A Bluetooth Low Energy controlled obstruction detecting bot, Microcontroller used was Arduino UNO, designed app for Controls on MIT App Inventor, used BLE Scanner to scan the BLE module HM-10.

1. The BOT uses UltraSonic sensor HC-SR04, BLE module HM-10, Microcontroller Arduino Uno and the Motor Driver L293D. Project aimed at designing an obstacle detection BOT controlled by BLE via mobile app.

2. After the app is connected to the bot via bluetooth, its movements can be controlled by the controls present on the app.

3. The Bot detects any obstruction present by HC-SR04, sends an ultrasonic pulse out at 40kHz and in presence of an obstacle wave bounces back to the sensor.

4. By calculating the travel time and the speed of sound, the obstacle distance is calculated . Minimum safe distance maintained of 15 centimeters.
