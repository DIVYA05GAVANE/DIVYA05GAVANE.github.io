BLE-Controlled Obstacle-Detecting Bot

Arduino UNO, HC-SR04, HM-10, L293D, MIT App Inventor

Overview

Built a Bluetooth Low Energy controlled obstacle-detecting robot. A mobile app controls movement through an HM-10 BLE link while an HC-SR04 measures obstacle distance and maintains a 15 cm safety threshold through Arduino UNO and L293D motor control.

Tools and methods

Arduino UNO, HC-SR04, HM-10, L293D, MIT App Inventor
